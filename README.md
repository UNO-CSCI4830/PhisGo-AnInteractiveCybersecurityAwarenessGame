PhisGo is an interactive cybersecurity education game that teaches players to spot online threats by putting them in realistic situations. Players enter Phishland and manage a fictional inbox, where they get a mix of normal emails and phishing attempts; so of which are fake password resets, and suspicious links. Each decision has a consequence, such as lost points or a compromised account, but these consequences are followed by feedback that explains the warning signs they missed. Players can also track their cybersecurity score, missions, and progress on a personal dashboard.
